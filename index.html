Morning Stretch and Plank Routine

About ten minutes on the floor: spine movement, three plank holds and three stretches.

Trainer is an original fictional character. Rest gaps add get-ready and switch-side time. The 10 s rest between front planks always stays. Left-side holds show the trainer mirrored.

How to do each move

    When to stop

    Stop at once and get medical help for chest pain or tightness, unusual breathlessness or dizziness. Tell Dr Anastasius you're doing daily planks.

    • Sharp pain, or pain, numbness or tingling down a leg: stop that move and skip it next time.
    • An ache lasting over an hour, or worse next morning: ease off that move for a few days.
    • Gout flare in a big toe: keep that foot flat when kneeling and plank on your knees. In a knee: do only the lying moves.

    Routine complete

    All seven moves done. Roll to your side and get up slowly.

    Dave's 5

    Five stretches, each held for 30 seconds. A full run of all five counts as one completion. The aim is 10 completions a week — for example two runs a day, Monday to Friday.
